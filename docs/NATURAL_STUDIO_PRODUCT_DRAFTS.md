## Draft license review

The current personal-use license is explicitly a draft for owner and legal review. It describes personal household printing and prohibits resale, redistribution, sublicensing, commercial use, and print-on-demand resale without a separate written license. Before use, confirm the legal seller identity, rights-holder and authority, local digital-content consent/cancellation requirements, support/refund process, accessibility, and the exact accepted license version to retain with an order.
